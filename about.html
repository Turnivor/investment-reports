What this is

Personal stock and industry research, written with AI tools and shared with a small group of invited friends. Friends can read the reports and ask for a company or an industry to be researched.

The reports are not investment advice. They are AI-assisted research and should be checked before acting on them.

Who can get in

Only people invited by the site owner. You sign in with your Google account or with a one-time code sent to your email address. If you have not been invited, the sign-in will not let you through.
